Who we are

RockSteadyFest is a co-op of local artists creating alcohol-free, substance-free spaces for music and arts in Marin County.

Our events are built to support connection, wellness, and positive community engagement for youth, families, and people in recovery who are often excluded from traditional music events.

From intimate acoustic nights to outdoor community concerts across Marin County.
